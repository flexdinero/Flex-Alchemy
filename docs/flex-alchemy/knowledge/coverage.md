The narrow verification result covers the four navigation links in the [first run](packs/repository-verification/first-run.md). The [scope conflict exercise](scope-conflict-exercise.md) records a manual retrieval and decision case. Promote a family after the relevant source has an owner, version, allowed uses and review date, and the proposed application passes its check. Record the evidence locator and downgrade the status if the evidence expires.
